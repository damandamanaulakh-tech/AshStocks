Exact source: `AM.07.Parameter.Files.zip / AM 07 Parameter Files/India 10-Year Bond Yield Historical Data.csv` (136,482 bytes; member SHA-256 `f8900835eb99d042f0452672518d6e3e274b6a1cddcb3d37b054c5985903923a`). The 10,303,731-byte parent archive's size/hash were freshly matched to the pinned release inventory. The member was fully CRC-read and UTF-8/BOM decoded.

All **2,551 rows and all six fields per row** were parsed: `Date`, `Price`, `Open`, `High`, `Low`, `Change %`. Real coverage is **1 January 2016–8 June 2026**, not fifteen years. Dates are unique, strictly descending, with no duplicate rows, width errors, missing cells, invalid numeric cells or nonpositive Price/Open/High/Low values.

## Quality and meaning

| Finding | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Dates parse as **month/day/year**. Day-first fails on 1,538 rows and changes 924 of the 1,013 rows it can parse. | A day-first parser would materially corrupt dates. Use this source's format explicitly, unlike the day-first macro CSV. |
| Price ranges 5.760–8.182. Filename/magnitude suggest yield percent, but the four value-column headers do not declare units. | Treat percent-yield units as an inference until source confirmation. No issuer/ISIN/benchmark-roll or maturity definition is present. |
| All 2,550 adjacent-observation `Change %` values reconcile to `100 × (Price / previous available Price − 1)` at two decimals. | This is relative percentage change in the source value, **not** a basis-point change in yield, bond-price return or total return. The first row in chronological history has no prior observation for validation. |
| **22 rows put Price below Low**, all 7 March–3 July 2025; maximum distance 0.011 source units. | Preserve and flag the source discrepancy. Do not validate this as ordinary OHLC envelopes or silently repair Low/Price. Underlying quote conventions remain unknown. |
| **456 rows have identical Price/Open/High/Low**, including the first 435 observations through 5 October 2017. | Intraday range meaning/availability cannot be assumed; these may be close-like observations or another source convention. |
| Twelve weekend observations; 183 missing weekdays; 82 gaps longer than three calendar days, maximum six days. | These are calendar diagnostics, not verified missing exchange sessions or invalid trades. Holidays/session and benchmark methodology were not looked up online. |
